Double-Entry Accounting

Every transfer generates immutable debit and credit entries guaranteeing Σ signed entries = 0. External clearing accounts ensure total journal balance.

🔒

Deadlock-Free Row Locking

Accounts are locked in strictly ascending numerical ID order under pessimistic write locks, eliminating cyclic deadlocks during high-concurrency transfers.

⚡

Cryptographic Idempotency

SHA-256 payload fingerprinting and unique database key constraints ensure safe retries, payload collision detection, and zero double-spending.

🛡️

Role-Based Access Control

Strict separation of duties between Account Holder, Administrator, and read-only Compliance Auditor.

📜

Immutable Audit Trail

Autonomous, isolated audit logging (REQUIRES_NEW) records both successful executions and refused attempts with client IP and forensic details.

📊

Running Balance Statements

Point-in-time financial statements with running balances, verified against ledger entries for complete auditability.
